A world to explore. A life to shape. A history to inherit.

The Ever-Unfolding Saga is a character-focused dark-fantasy RPG about finding your own path through a dangerous, changing world. Travel between places, meet people with their own memories and loyalties, face threats, and decide which opportunities deserve your attention.

Explore and make your own way

Move through a procedurally generated world of connected locations, distinct encounters, and discoveries. Travel, investigate dangerous places, search forgotten remains, talk to characters, take on quests, and follow the leads that interest you. Time passes as you act, and the time of day can affect the atmosphere and the people you can meet.

People remember

Your actions become part of the world's history. Important events are recorded, characters can remember what happened and react differently when you meet again, and your standing with factions reflects your deeds. The world is not just a backdrop for the current character.

Death is not the end of the world's story

When a character dies, you can start over with a reset world or continue in the world they left behind. Previous lives can leave traces: remembered events, persistent remains, discoveries, and consequences for whoever comes next.

Develop your character

Gain experience through your actions, improve your attributes, manage health and conditions, and build an inventory from what you find and earn. Quests, encounters, equipment, and character progression give each journey its own shape.

Explore in different ways

The game combines a graphical interface with direct spatial exploration in selected locations. These areas support eight-direction movement, obstacles, environmental props, and points of interest. Desktop movement is keyboard-driven; Android uses an on-screen virtual joystick. Combat currently uses turn-based encounters.

Extend the game with mods

Campaign content is loaded from structured data, and mods can add or replace game content. Mods can also include their own visual assets, allowing the base game and community-made additions to share the same content system.

Current state: The game is in active development. This page describes features that are implemented in the current project, not a list of promised future features.
